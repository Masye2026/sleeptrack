Health-data guide

Heart rate: beats per minute; readings can be affected by fit and movement.

SpO₂: an estimate of blood oxygen; consumer sensor readings may be inaccurate.

Sleep stages: estimates inferred from available signals, not certainty.

Missing data: means unavailable, not normal.

SleepTrack cannot diagnose sleep apnea, insomnia, or any other medical condition. Speak with a qualified healthcare professional about health concerns.
